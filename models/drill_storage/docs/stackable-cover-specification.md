# Stackable drill-storage covers

The existing smooth PETG covers remain available. Each of the five 1×1 drill-storage sets also has a separate stackable cover, retaining the existing collar/snap interface and engraved set label.

- **SC-1 — Stacking:** The top of each stackable cover has an approximately 4.4 mm stacking lip above the 7 mm-unit body datum. Its socket receives the full 4.4 mm of the existing 1×1 foot (0.7 mm lower bevel, 1.8 mm straight band, 1.9 mm upper bevel), so a seated holder advances by whole 7 mm units. Only the stackable cover's lip widens to 42 mm (about 0.25 mm per side beyond the 41.5 mm body); the foot socket retains the PETG sliding fit. Source: user choice of the draft [Gridfinity design-reference drawing](https://gridfinity.xyz/specification/), whose 2U example totals 18.4 mm (14 + 4.4), with full foot seating.
- **SC-2 — Compatibility and exception:** Smooth covers, original 41.5 mm bodies and bases (including their snap joints and foot profile) remain unchanged. The stackable variants fit the same collars and keep their existing minimum tool-tip clearance. The 42 mm lip consumes the entire nominal 42 mm grid pitch, and its 41.72 mm sliding-fit mouth leaves only about 0.14 mm wall per side: this is an experimental, printer-dependent exception to the 0.8 mm printable-wall rule, not an assertion of strength, successful slicing or adjacent-cell clearance under tolerances. Source: user decision to prioritize the draft profile while retaining existing bases.
- **SC-3 — Two-part print only:** All seven split-capable leaves (`wood.cover_stackable`, `metal.cover_stackable`, `stone.cover_stackable`, `allen.cover_stackable`, `hex.bits.cover_stackable`, `dremel.cover_stackable` and `bin.lid`, under `drill_storage`) print only as two PETG children. Remove the public one-piece alternative and the `support` and `separate_stacking_lips` switches. Smooth covers remain unchanged and available; sideways holders are excluded. Source: user clarification and accepted purpose/decision delta, 2026-10-23. This supersedes the 2026-09-28 supported one-piece default and 2026-09-30 optional-split decision.
- **SC-4 — Located, retained glue joint:** Return a non-assembly named Compound with exactly `lid_body` and `stacking_lips`, bed-seated and XY-separated for print preview; each child downloads as its own STL. Keep all receivers/webs in one connected lip child. Improve the former flat-on-flat socket-floor joint with keyed positive location, light self-retention during adhesive cure, and adhesive relief. Preserve the accepted foot fit, stacking pitch, solid roof/tool clearance, labels and closure; do not redesign bases or inserts. Source: user's accepted keyed-location/light-retention/glue-relief proposal, 2026-10-23. Implementation dimensions and CAD evidence belong to the current contracts, not an assumed fit or strength claim.

Physical printer-to-printer fit, the thin lip's slicing and durability, and interference between adjacent holders need printed-part acceptance before loading a stack. Two-part printing removes the unsupported socket roof, not the experimental thin-wall exception. Adhesive compatibility, cured bond strength, printed flatness, locating/retention effort, glue relief and loaded-stack durability remain physically unverified. Historical support-removal and planar-split evidence does not establish acceptance of the new joint. Original smooth covers remain the unchanged no-glue alternative.
