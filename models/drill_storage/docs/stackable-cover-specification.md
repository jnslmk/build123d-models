# Stackable drill-storage covers

The existing smooth PETG covers remain available. Each of the five 1×1 drill-storage sets also has a separate stackable cover, retaining the existing collar/snap interface and engraved set label.

- **SC-1 — Stacking:** The top of each stackable cover has an approximately 4.4 mm stacking lip above the 7 mm-unit body datum. Its socket receives the full 4.4 mm of the existing 1×1 foot (0.7 mm lower bevel, 1.8 mm straight band, 1.9 mm upper bevel), so a seated holder advances by whole 7 mm units. Only the stackable cover's lip widens to 42 mm (about 0.25 mm per side beyond the 41.5 mm body); the foot socket retains the PETG sliding fit. Source: user choice of the draft [Gridfinity design-reference drawing](https://gridfinity.xyz/specification/), whose 2U example totals 18.4 mm (14 + 4.4), with full foot seating.
- **SC-2 — Compatibility and exception:** Smooth covers, original 41.5 mm bodies and bases (including their snap joints and foot profile) remain unchanged. The stackable variants fit the same collars and keep their existing minimum tool-tip clearance. The 42 mm lip consumes the entire nominal 42 mm grid pitch, and its 41.72 mm sliding-fit mouth leaves only about 0.14 mm wall per side: this is an experimental, printer-dependent exception to the 0.8 mm printable-wall rule, not an assertion of strength, successful slicing or adjacent-cell clearance under tolerances. Source: user decision to prioritize the draft profile while retaining existing bases.
- **SC-3 — Default print:** By default, stackable covers remain one-piece PETG prints, top-down with the mouth up. Expose removable breakaway socket support as a Boolean parameter on all five covers, enabled by default. Disabling it removes only the sacrificial lattice and attachment nibs, leaving the cover and socket unchanged; use slicer support or verify the unsupported bridge before printing without it. Source: user request to add support directly to the model, revised by user confirmation 2026-09-28. Remove built-in support and all attachment nibs before stacking.
- **SC-4 — Optional separate stacking lips:** Expose `separate_stacking_lips`, a Boolean labelled **Separate stacking lips**, default false, on the five upright sets' stackable covers, the Dremel stackable cover and the bin lid; sideways covers are excluded. Split the clean finished lid at the socket-floor plane: retain the complete roof and existing closure/labels in `lid_body`, and all receiver walls in `stacking_lips`. Return a non-assembly named Compound with exactly these two children, each bed-seated at z=0 and separated in XY only for preview. Print the body roof/glue-face-down and the lips glue-face-down/socket-up, without sacrificial supports regardless of the support toggle. Each child exports as its own STL; all multi-cell receiver walls share one `stacking_lips` STL, with the original intervening webs retaining their spacing. Flat mating faces glue together with no designed adhesive gap or extra alignment features: dry-align the outer contours, preserve any half-cell socket arrangement, and keep adhesive out of the receivers. The ideal zero-gap assembled geometry, stacking pitch, labels and closure fit equal the clean one-piece lid. Source: user purpose and decision confirmation, 2026-09-30.

Physical printer-to-printer fit, the thin lip's slicing and durability, support removal, and interference between adjacent holders need printed-part acceptance before loading a stack. Split printing removes the unsupported socket roof, not the experimental thin-wall exception. Actual adhesive choice, cured bond strength, bed-face flatness, bond-line thickness and loaded-stack durability remain physically unverified; any real bond-line thickness increases the stacking height. The original smooth covers remain the support-free, no-glue alternative.
