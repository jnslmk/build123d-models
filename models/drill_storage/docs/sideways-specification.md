# Sideways wood and metal drill storage

- **SD-1 — Separate variants:** Derive new Gridfinity holders for the complete `sets.WOOD` and `sets.METAL` tool lists; leave the existing upright holders and their parts unchanged. Source: user request and purpose/delta confirmation, 2026-09-28.
- **SD-2 — Horizontal tools:** Store the drill bits with their long axes horizontal, turned 90° from the existing upright holders. The holders must be printable in their Gridfinity foot-down pose. Source: user request and confirmation, 2026-09-28.
- **SD-3 — Smallest feasible envelope:** Target one cell across by three cells along and 5U high; use four cells along only if three cannot fit the complete tool set, and 6U high only if 5U cannot fit it. Gridfinity pitch is 42 mm and a height unit is 7 mm. Source: user request and confirmation, 2026-09-28; `box.py`.
- **SD-4 — Side-opening cover:** The holders resemble the upright drill holders laid on their sides: the PETG cover slides axially over a collar on the ASA base instead of using the former dovetail rail and sleeve. It remains removable laterally to expose the bits. Source: user redesign request and purpose/delta confirmation, 2026-09-28.
- **SD-5 — Cover owns forward feet:** The exposed long platform and its two wood / three metal forward Gridfinity feet belong to the removable cover, not the fixed rear guide. The rear guide remains on its own single-cell foot. The assembled holder must be lifted out of the baseplate before the cover is pulled sideways; feet engaged in neighbouring baseplate cells cannot slide laterally. Source: user-highlighted image, explicit acceptance of the off-baseplate opening sequence, 2026-09-28.

- **SD-6 — Full-foot stacking:** Both assembled sideways holders receive a matching foot in every cell across their 1×3 / 1×4 top, including the rear cell owned by the ASA guide. Seat the complete 4.4 mm Gridfinity foot with nominal sliding clearance at a whole-5U (35 mm) stacking pitch. A 42 mm receiving lip and its thin mouth are experimental as on the upright stackable covers; printed fit, adjacent-cell clearance, and load-bearing strength require a physical trial. Keep the foot-down, side-opening architecture and the upright variants unchanged. Source: user request and purpose/delta confirmation, 2026-09-28.
- **SD-7 — Set-name cover and tool map on base:** Keep WOOD / METAL engraved on the PETG cover. Replace the base's set-name label with individual drill sizes and CSK / TAP / STEP identifiers, arranged as a legible map of the guide positions on the ASA rear face. Keep lettering within the solid back wall and clear of its rounded corners. Source: user correction and purpose/delta confirmation, 2026-09-28.
- **SD-8 — TPU grip cartridges:** Each sideways wood and metal holder has a separately printable TPU insert gripping the plain shanks, seated in its ASA guide. ASA bores guide without gripping; the complete named tool set remains accessible after removing the side-opening cover. Source: user redesign request and purpose/delta confirmation, 2026-09-28.
- **SD-9 — Flush ASA exterior:** Keep the exposed side of the rear ASA guide continuous through the cartridge-seat and collar transitions, and carry its lower outer edge flush to the PETG bed at the assembled joint. Preserve the removable cover's sliding clearance, guide bores, cartridge seat, and print pose. Source: user-highlighted seam and purpose/delta confirmation, 2026-09-28.

The rear guide, TPU grip cartridge and foot-bearing cover are separately printable, with one stacking receiver per foot position on the exposed top surfaces of the rigid parts. The original upright ASA/TPU/PETG parts remain independent and unchanged.
