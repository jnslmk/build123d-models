# Keep Stella aluminium profiles unmodified

On 2026-09-20, the user explicitly ruled out modifications to the aluminium lamp
profiles while accepting independently replaceable lamps. This rejects a drilled
retention hole even though it could provide a positive axial stop: the connector
design must instead establish adequate non-invasive retention without crushing
the thin profile or relying on the diffuser as a structural attachment. This is
an accepted design boundary, not acceptance of a retention mechanism or an
overhead-use safety claim; the active requirements remain in the [Stella
specification](../stella-specification.md), and the current execution boundary is
the [CAD contract](../cad-contract.md).
