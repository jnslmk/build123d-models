# Seat Stella arms on broad keyed faces and clamp with two M3 screws

The user accepted this arrangement in interview Round 4 on 2026-09-20: each arm
seats on a broad, flat core face with substantial locating shoulders and is
clamped by two M3 screws into core-mounted Ø4 × 5 mm heat-set inserts. Compared
with one larger screw and small locating keys, this places the design emphasis on
the printed bearing surfaces and rotation restraint while using the available
inserts; the trade-off is two screws per arm and a wider seating area. This
selects the joint architecture, not its capacity, pilot diameter, or final screw
length; the durable requirement is in the [Stella specification](../stella-specification.md),
and dimensions, analytical assumptions, and the core-first boundary remain in
the [CAD contract](../cad-contract.md).
